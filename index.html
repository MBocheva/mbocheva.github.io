Hi — my name is Michaela and I'm a Yale PhD student in cognitive science. I study perception of structure and randomness, in both vision and higher-order cognition.

I'm specifically interested in the nature of structure representations across various domains, such as low-level scene perception, agentic motion, and action.
Check out our lab's website at https://perception.yale.edu/
